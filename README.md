## Key highlights

🎯 **Customise everything:** move the menu to any side, rename and reorder sections, pick fonts, card styles, corners and your own accent colour. Go further with your own CSS and HTML.

⚡ **Gamified:** earn XP by completing quests, building habit streaks and hitting PRs to unlock hidden achievements and the arcade.

🏁 **Your day as a lap:** the Today screen is a running track that fills up as your day gets done.

📅 **School-ready:** timetable, calendar, countdowns and a timetable home screen widget.

⏱️ **Focus timer:** a Pomodoro timer that keeps running in the background.

🏋️ **Sports and gym tracking:** log training, track PRs and see your progress.

📝 **Notes and journal:** rich notes, handwriting, a journal and quick mood check-ins.

🎨 **15 themes:** 11 original Frutiger themes (designed with the help of AI) plus clean classics, each with matching widgets and its own app icon.

🧩 **Mods and a setup gallery:** add taskbars, docks and animated backgrounds with sandboxed scripts, then share your setup or try other people's in one tap.

👥 **Friends and chat:** optional accounts for friends, chat and cloud sync.

🔒 **Private by default:** everything is stored on your device unless you choose to sign in.

## Install

1. Download the latest **Qalm APK** from [Releases](https://github.com/lootblox0-lang/Qalm/releases/latest).
2. Open the file. If your phone asks, allow installing apps from your browser or files app, then tap **Install**.
3. Already have FLUX? Install Qalm over it. Your data stays.

## Formerly FLUX

Qalm used to be called **FLUX Life OS**. There were already lots of apps called Flux, so it got a new name that's short, simple and all its own. Same app, same data.
